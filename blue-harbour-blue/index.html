Blue Harbour Blue

Australia

The first luxury vodka from Australia is created with uncompromising quality, outstanding ingredients, innovative design and close to nature.

Our Blue Harbour Blue is a vodka crafted in a multiple-plate column copper still from Australian wheat, filtered five times, diluted with fresh Australian spring water and hand bottled, resulting in the cleanest and softest spirit with hints of orange peel and citrus notes.

Blue Harbour Blue is an ultra-soft sipping vodka, best enjoyed neat or on the rocks.

40% Alc. by Vol. · 80 Proof

700 / 750mL
