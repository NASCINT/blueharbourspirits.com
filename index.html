Welcome

Welcome to Blue Harbour

The Blue Harbour, with her tides and estuaries, her islands and beaches, has always been the beating heart of Australia.

Blue Harbour Spirits capture her magic in a bottle: made from home-grown Australian wheat, filtered multiple times and blended with the purest Australian spring water, for the cleanest, softest, uniquely Australian super-premium spirits.

Made for sundowners at the yacht club, cocktails on a rooftop bar, and any occasion worth sharing.

Blue Harbour Cocktails

For any occasion worth sharing

Four signature serves, built around Blue Harbour Vodka and our limited-release truffle vodka.

See all recipes →

Harbour Thyme Martini

  • 60ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 2 sprigs of thyme
  • 15ml lemon juice
  • 2 dashes lemon bitters
  • 15ml soda water

Stir and strain into a chilled martini glass. Garnish with long lemon zest and a fresh sprig of thyme.

Black Queen of France

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Truffle Vodka
  • 15ml VSOP cognac
  • 3 dashes cherry bitters
  • 5ml sugar syrup
  • Top with dry French champagne

Rim the glass with icing sugar, sink a cherry to the bottom, then top with champagne.

Blue Harbour Martini

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 30ml Blue Harbour Black Truffle Vodka
  • 2 sprigs of sage
  • 5ml dry vermouth

Stir to chill, double strain, and garnish with sage resting in the drink.

Harbour Espresso Royal

  • 45ml Blue Harbour Black Truffle Vodka
  • 15ml Wild Wombat Coffee Liqueur
  • 15ml espresso

Hard shake for a nice froth, pour into a chilled latte glass and dust with cinnamon.
