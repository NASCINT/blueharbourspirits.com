Blue Harbour Gold

Australia

The first luxury whisky from Australia is created with uncompromising quality, outstanding ingredients, innovative design and close to nature.

Our Blue Harbour Gold is a whisky crafted in a multiple-plate column copper still from Australian barley, filtered five times, diluted with fresh Australian spring water and hand bottled, resulting in the cleanest and softest spirit.

Blue Harbour Gold is an ultra-soft sipping whisky, best enjoyed neat or on the rocks.

40% Alc. by Vol. · 80 Proof

700 / 750mL
