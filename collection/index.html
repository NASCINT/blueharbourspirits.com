The Collection

Synonymous with luxury and perfection, Blue Harbour Spirits is more than a brand; it is a lifestyle, representing exceptional quality, stylish indulgence and superior craftsmanship. The range represents the blue sky and waters of Sydney, the relaxed Australian way of life, and of course, sailing.

Vodka

Blue Harbour Blue

Australia

Crafted in a multiple-plate column copper still from Australian wheat, filtered five times, diluted with fresh Australian spring water and hand bottled — resulting in the cleanest, softest spirit with hints of orange peel and citrus notes. An ultra-soft sipping vodka, best drunk neat or on the rocks.
