Blue Harbour Red

Australia

The first luxury baijiu from Australia is created with uncompromising quality, outstanding ingredients, innovative design and close to nature.

Our Blue Harbour Red is a baijiu crafted in a multiple-plate column copper still from Australian wheat, sorghum, rice and barley, filtered five times, diluted with fresh Australian spring water and hand bottled, resulting in the cleanest and softest spirit.

Blue Harbour Red is an ultra-complex sipping baijiu, best enjoyed neat or on the rocks.

53% Alc. by Vol. · 106 Proof

700mL
