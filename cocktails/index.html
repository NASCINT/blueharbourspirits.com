Cocktails

Perfect in a sundowner at the yacht club, sipped on board a cruiser at dusk as the sun dips below the Harbour Bridge, or at a rooftop bar as the lights of the Blue Harbour twinkle around you. In fact, for any occasion worth sharing.

Blue Harbour Martini

Ingredients

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 30ml Blue Harbour Black Truffle Vodka
  • 2 sprigs of sage
  • 5ml dry vermouth

Garnish

A sprig of sage.

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a mixing glass and stir to chill.
  2. Double strain into a martini glass.
  3. Garnish with the sage resting in the drink and serve.

Harbour Thyme Martini

Ingredients

  • 60ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 2 sprigs of thyme
  • 15ml lemon juice
  • 2 dashes of lemon bitters
  • 15ml soda water

Garnish

Long lemon zest and a fresh sprig of thyme.

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a mixing glass and stir to mix and chill.
  2. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  3. Garnish and serve.

Harbour Breeze

Ingredients

  • 45ml Blue Harbour Black Truffle Vodka
  • 30ml pineapple juice
  • Mint sprig

Garnish

Large mint sprig.

Method

  1. Pour all ingredients into a Boston shaker and dry shake.
  2. Add ice and shake again to chill.
  3. Double strain into a martini glass and garnish.

Harbour Heaven

Ingredients

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 15ml Blue Harbour Truffle Vodka
  • 15ml lime juice
  • 15ml pineapple juice
  • 15ml agave nectar

Garnish

Two pineapple leaves and a cherry speared on a small toothpick.

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a Boston shaker and shake.
  2. Double strain into a chilled martini glass.
  3. Add garnish and serve.

Harbour Espresso

Ingredients

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 30ml Wild Wombat Coffee Liqueur
  • 30ml espresso

Garnish

Grated cinnamon over the top of the froth.

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a Boston shaker and hard shake to achieve a nice layer of froth.
  2. Pour into a chilled latte glass and dust the top of the froth with cinnamon.

Harbour Espresso Royal

Ingredients

  • 45ml Blue Harbour Black Truffle Vodka
  • 15ml Wild Wombat Coffee Liqueur
  • 15ml espresso

Garnish

Grated cinnamon over the top of the froth.

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a Boston shaker and hard shake to achieve a nice layer of froth.
  2. Pour into a chilled latte glass and dust the top of the froth with cinnamon.

Black Queen of France

Ingredients

  • 30ml Blue Harbour Truffle Vodka
  • 15ml VSOP cognac
  • 3 dashes of cherry bitters
  • 5ml sugar syrup
  • Top with dry French champagne

Garnish

Sunken cherry and a sugar rim.

Method

  1. Rim the top of the glass with icing sugar.
  2. Add the cherry bitters to the glass.
  3. Add the spirits and sugar syrup straight into the flute.
  4. Sink the cherry to the bottom of the glass.
  5. Top with French champagne.

El Jefe's Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 45ml Blue Harbour Vodka
  • 15ml Wild Wombat Coffee Liqueur
  • 15ml chocolate syrup
  • 15ml freshly brewed espresso
  • 15ml fresh cream or coconut cream

Garnish

Three coffee beans. Rim the glass with cocoa powder.

Method

  1. Place the ingredients in a shaker full of ice and shake vigorously.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass with a cocoa-powder rim.
