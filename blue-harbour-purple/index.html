Blue Harbour Purple

Australia

The first luxury gin from Australia is created with uncompromising quality, outstanding ingredients, innovative design and close to nature.

Our Blue Harbour Purple is a gin crafted in a multiple-plate column copper still from Australian wheat, filtered five times and diluted with fresh Australian spring water, resulting in the cleanest and softest spirit, then infused with our secret recipe of fresh and dry botanicals.

Blue Harbour Purple is an ultra-soft sipping gin, best enjoyed neat or on the rocks.

42% Alc. by Vol. · 84 Proof

700 / 750mL
